Features

Agents for the work that piles up at the front desk

Start with a pack for your line of work, then turn agents on or off. Each one shows what it did and waits for you where it matters.

Receptionist

Answers new leads and routine questions from your price list, hours and policies, and offers free slots from your calendar.

Missed-call text-back

Texts every missed caller within a minute and opens a callback task for the front desk.

Reminders

Reminds clients the day before and handles confirm and reschedule replies, so fewer appointments are missed.

Collections

Follows up on unpaid invoices politely, using what you already agreed with the client, such as payment plans.

Reviews

Asks happy clients for a review, drafts replies, and tells you about any review of three stars or less.

Morning brief

At 08:00: what happened yesterday, today's bookings, money at risk, and who needs you first.

How it works

Set up in an afternoon

No new phone system or CRM. Computo works with the tools you already use.

1

Pick your pack

Clinic, law firm or insurance agent. It sets up the agents, message templates and the questions they can answer.

2

Connect your inbox and calendar

Email, calendar and your phone system's call log. Your price list and policies become what the agents answer from.

3

Approve from your phone

Drafts arrive with the reason and sources. Approve, edit or discard. You decide which agents may send on their own later.

FAQ

Questions

Is Computo available today?

Not yet. Early access opens soon for a small group of offices; a sign-up form is on its way to this page.

Will it message my clients on its own?

Only if you allow it. Every agent starts with drafts that you approve. Later you can let a specific agent send a specific kind of message, such as appointment reminders, and turn it off at any time.

Where does our client data live?

On your own computer or your own private server. Call audio is transcribed locally. Text sent to the AI model is masked first (ID, card and phone numbers) and goes only to a paid model plan that doesn't train on it.

Do I need to replace my CRM or phone system?

No. Computo reads from and drafts into the tools you already have. It isn't another CRM.

Does it work in Hebrew?

Yes. Every screen works in Hebrew and English, and agents reply in the client's language.

What will it cost?

One simple monthly price per office, announced before early access opens.
