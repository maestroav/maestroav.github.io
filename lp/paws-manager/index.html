FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I import data from my old software or spreadsheets?

Yes! You can easily import your existing client and pet records via CSV. Our support team is also happy to assist with custom data migration free of charge on the Professional plan.

Do pet parents need to download an app to book appointments?

No download required. Pet parents can book appointments, view vaccination records, and receive updates through a clean, mobile-friendly web portal branded with your store's name.

Can I manage retail inventory alongside boarding and grooming?

Absolutely. PawsManager combines point-of-sale retail inventory with boarding and grooming management so you have a single source of truth for your entire business.

Is there a contract or setup fee?

No contracts and no setup fees. All plans are month-to-month, and you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time with a single click.
